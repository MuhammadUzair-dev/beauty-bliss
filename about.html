My Story

From Pharmacy to Passion

Hi, my name is Dr. Butool Kazmi. Every business starts with one person with a dream. For the longest time, I thought my dream was to become a pharmacist. I went through years of schooling, various exams, and licensures. Eventually, I realized I wanted something more from life.

So here we are. After months of deliberation, I gathered the courage to move towards my passion. My love for makeup spearheaded the decision that led to the creation of Beauty Bliss USA.

Beauty Bliss is rooted in the belief that every individual deserves to look and feel beautiful — whether with no makeup, a little makeup, or glamourous makeup. Here, no person goes without glowing with joy internally and externally.

To take it further, I became a certified makeup artist and a licensed skincare specialist. From personalized skincare routines and monthly treatments to skin prepping and makeup — I want to be there every step of the way to make sure you feel, well, like yourself.

When your skincare specialist is also your makeup artist... your skin is in good hands.

The Meaning Behind Our Logo

Why the Butterfly?

The logo for Beauty Bliss is inspired by the ethereal beauty of butterflies. Butterflies are symbolic of transformation, growth, and the beauty that emerges from within.

Just like how skincare routines, treatments, and makeup help enhance one's features, the process of a caterpillar transforming into a butterfly represents the journey of self-care and self-love.

The logo combines the delicateness of a butterfly with the sparkle of glitter and the softness of clouds — representing the transformative and uplifting experience of Beauty Bliss.
